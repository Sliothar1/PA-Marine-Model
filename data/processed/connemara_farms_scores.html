Connemara Farms — weekly Dinophysis risk

Grower / co-op dashboard. Research nowcasts from sea-surface temperature (NOAA OISST) plus season and location — not an official Marine Institute warning. Always follow official HAB / biotoxin notices before harvesting.

How to read a score: calibrated probability that Dinophysis will be at or above 100 cells per litre in the current or next monitoring week (0–2 week nowcast). Compare to the “seasonal usual” for that week of year. Bands are communication aids for growers — not regulatory cut-offs.

Risk bands (plain English)

Higher ≥ 15%
Elevated chance of Dinophysis ≥ 100 cells/L in this or next week. Check MI bulletins before harvest plans.
Moderate 7–15%
Some seasonal / SST-linked risk. Watch bulletins and recent cell counts.
Lower < 7%
Below typical risk for this week-of-year, or quiet conditions. Still follow official notices.

  • Lehanagh (NMP) (sparse_historical): Only 25 station-weeks (2009–2020); no recent HAB samples. Kept in set for site continuity; not used for grower "latest week" tables unless data reappears. Nearby Lehanagh Pool sentinel buoy is separate.

  • Lehanagh buoy (buoy_only): NRT from May 2024; no published QC delayed-mode set. Context only — not scored for HAB exceedance.
  • Rosmuc OISST nulls: HAB labels exist, but satellite SST is missing at the station pixel — scores there lean on week-of-year and location only.
  • Lehanagh NMP: sparse / historical-only (ends ~2020); excluded from the latest grower table. The nearby Lehanagh buoy is separate and buoy-only.
  • Mace Head / Lehanagh buoys: buoy-only hydrography — no Dinophysis labels, not scored for HAB exceedance.
  • Closure-risk proxy: national area-closed model (all toxins + admin rules), only where toxin/status join exists; often “—” when toxin ingest lags phyto.
  • Irregular sampling: a missing week is “not sampled”, not a true negative.
